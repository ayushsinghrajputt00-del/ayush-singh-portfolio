Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the personal portfolio website of Ayush Singh.

1. Information this website receives

This website does not provide account registration, visitor profiles, advertising forms or an on-site message database. It does not intentionally ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information.

If you use the contact form, your browser opens your chosen email application with a message draft addressed to Ayush Singh. The website itself does not submit that message to a server. If you send the email, your email provider processes it and the message is received in the recipient's mailbox.

2. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted using Vercel. Like other hosting providers, Vercel may process technical request information in server and security logs, such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, timestamps and diagnostic information, to deliver, secure and maintain the service. Vercel's handling of this information is subject to its own privacy documentation and terms.

Read the Vercel Privacy Policy.

3. Fonts and external services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page requests those font files, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. The use of Google Fonts is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

The website may also link to external services such as GitHub and Instagram. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Following an external link takes you away from this website.

4. Cookies and analytics

The portfolio code does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run third-party analytics. Hosting or external services may use essential technical mechanisms under their own policies. No claim is made about cookies or data practices on websites linked from this portfolio.

5. How information is used

Technical hosting information may be used by the hosting provider to operate, protect and troubleshoot the website. Emails that you choose to send may be used to respond to your enquiry and maintain the correspondence.

6. Data retention and your choices

The website does not maintain a server-side contact form database. Emails you send are handled by the email providers involved and may remain in their systems or mailboxes according to their retention settings. You can choose not to use the contact links or external services.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact
